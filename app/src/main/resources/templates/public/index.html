Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up and governing your TopLMS workspace.

How does multi-tenant data isolation work in TopLMS? ▼

TopLMS enforces strict row-level partitioning with a tenant_id on every data entity in PostgreSQL. All HTTP requests are resolved to your workspace subdomain via TenantInterceptor and verified against user credentials, completely preventing any cross-tenant data visibility.

Can instructors create both video lectures and quizzes? ▼

Yes. The Instructor Curriculum Studio supports hierarchical course structures consisting of modules, rich video lectures, downloadable syllabus resources, multiple-choice quizzes, and deadline-gated homework assignments.

How do students access their academy workspace? ▼

Learners can either self-register on your academy’s dedicated subdomain (e.g. demo.toplms.app/signup) or be directly enrolled by a Tenant Administrator or Instructor.

Can we upgrade our subscription plan as our student roster expands? ▼

Absolutely. You can start on the FREE tier and upgrade to GROWTH or Custom Enterprise at any time directly within your Tenant Admin workspace settings without any service interruption.

Is TopLMS compatible with existing university systems? ▼

Yes. TopLMS is built on standard Spring Boot 4 architecture, offering RESTful extensibility and clean data export mechanisms for institutional grading audits.
